Pilot 7 concerns how existing solutions for communication between home and kindergarten, after-school care and school can work better together, so that parents and guardians can have messages, calendar information and history in one place, and choose for themselves how to receive them. The pilot builds on reuse, interoperability and open interfaces, not on making a new app.
